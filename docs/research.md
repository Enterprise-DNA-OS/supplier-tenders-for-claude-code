# Target assessment: Market Dojo

Checked 14 October 2026. Family: strategic-sourcing. Product: Supplier Tenders for Claude Code. The registry contains purchasing, supplier assurance and supplier-side response writing, but no buyer-side tender evaluation build before this run.

## Bill first

Market Dojo's [pricing page](https://marketdojo.com/pricing/) lists Strategic Sourcing at GBP 2,000 monthly on the on-demand monthly option, excluding taxes. Twelve months at that rate is GBP 24,000. The same page lists GBP 667 monthly billed annually, a materially cheaper commitment. This comparison does not imply that annual customers pay the monthly on-demand rate.

The vendor's currency selector also offers USD, but the sourced price record uses the GBP displayed directly. No currency conversion, implementation charge, savings percentage or customer invoice has been invented.

## Search and buyer research

The search for Market Dojo alternatives returns comparison listings from [SelectHub](https://www.selecthub.com/procurement-software/market-dojo/alternatives/) and other directories. This shows an existing search result category, not measured search volume. Demand score 3 reflects that limit.

[User reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/111529/Market-Dojo/reviews/) include friction around supplier logins, alongside positive comments about value and support. The new page makes no broad claim that the incumbent is difficult, bad value, or unable to report the same questions.

## Verified product and migration facts

- [Vendor sourcing page](https://marketdojo.com/sourcing/): supplier events, weighted scoring, lots and spreadsheet import/export. These define the evaluation records, not a promise to reproduce the full service.
- [Export help](https://support.marketdojo.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-do-i-see-a-participants-answers-to-a-questionnaire): report downloads. Individual tenant headings are not documented here.
- [Analytics help](https://support.marketdojo.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-use-the-analytics-tab): spreadsheet reporting and a Lot Matrix. A pivoted matrix requires reshaping before the local long-row CSV importer.
- [NZ evaluation guidance](https://www.procurement.govt.nz/assets/procurement-property/documents/guide-evaluation-panel-chairs.pdf) and [Australian procurement process guidance](https://www.finance.gov.au/government/procurement/buying-australian-government/procurement-process-considerations) inform record controls. See compliance.md for scope.

## Build boundary

Internal supplier tender evaluation with received bids, fixed criteria, panel declarations, recorded scores, clarification follow-up and a human-approved decision snapshot. No live auction, supplier network, public portal, automated award, purchasing or payment. This is a useful standalone evaluation desk, with further customer workflows scoped through Omni by Enterprise DNA.
